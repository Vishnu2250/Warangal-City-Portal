About Warangal

Warangal is one of the major cities in Telangana with a rich history dating back to the Kakatiya dynasty. It is an important center for education, culture, and commerce in the region.

History

Warangal served as the capital of the Kakatiya dynasty in the 12th and 13th centuries. The city is known for its grand fort, intricate stone gateways, and ancient temples.

Modern City

Today, Warangal is a fast-growing urban center with universities, IT parks, and transport links connecting it to Hyderabad and other major cities.
